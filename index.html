Frequently asked questions

How does food scanning work?

Tap the + button, choose Scan Food, and take a photo of your meal. Forkcast identifies the food on your iPhone and estimates calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar, and sodium. If something's off, use “Fix with AI” to describe the difference.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

The AI meal descriptions and AI Coach use Apple Intelligence on supported devices. Without it, Forkcast uses its built-in food database, so you can still scan, search, and log meals.

Do I need an account?

No. Forkcast works without sign-in, and your data is stored only on your device.

How are my calorie and macro targets calculated?

From your age, sex, height, weight, activity level, and goal, using the Mifflin–St Jeor equation. You can adjust your details anytime in the Profile tab.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Profile › Delete All Data, or delete the app.

Is this medical advice?

No. Nutrition values are estimates. Talk to a healthcare professional before making significant changes to your diet.
