Forkcast Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Forkcast (“the app”) is built to keep your information on your device. This policy explains what the app handles and how.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Forkcast has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. The developer has no access to your data.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following locally on your iPhone:

  • Profile details you enter, such as age, sex, height, weight, activity level, and goals
  • Meals you log, including their nutrition values and any meal photos you take or choose
  • Water and weight entries

This data never leaves your device through Forkcast. If you use iCloud or device backups, Apple may include the app's data in your backups according to your device settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only to analyze meals you choose to scan. Images are processed on your device with Apple's Vision framework and are never uploaded.

Apple Intelligence

AI features, including meal descriptions and the AI Coach, use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Your prompts and food log are processed on your device and are not sent to the developer.

Deleting your data

Delete everything at any time from Profile › Delete All Data, or by deleting the app.

Children

Forkcast does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
